## Limitations

- Qualification ran on Linux. The Darwin process-group and Windows Job Object branches remain covered by their platform-gated tests but were not executable on this host.
- Upgrade recovery intentionally fails closed if more than 10,000 authoritative leased/running source Activations require backfill; no such production upgrade was performed because this beta migration has not shipped.
- No real provider request was manufactured solely to turn `unverified` into `reachable`; durable evidence changes only after an operator probe or genuine provider work.
- No production/development data, deployment, merge, or permanent backward-compatibility dual path was used. The schema-1 reader exists only to drain/remove the exact legacy authority before writing the sole schema-2 format.
