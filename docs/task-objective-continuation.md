# Whole-task continuity and concise collaboration

Bible 2 #1, #2, #11, #12, #13, #14, #17 and #23 apply. The later human
instruction to combine all implementation before consolidated validation wins
over the Bible's original per-fix test cadence.

## Task, batch and blocking outcome

The canonical human task remains the objective across delegated batches, provider
Runs, steering and context rollover. Batch completion and progress messages do
not complete that task. `taskDelivery: final` means the whole objective and its
required verification are complete, with tracked results integrated. Existing
dependency checks still reject a final outcome while a Decision or delegation is
outstanding.

`taskDelivery: blocked` requires an actionable canonical pending blocking
Decision for this exact task. Both the HTTP publication route and transactional
required-reply authority enforce it. A truthful status message about unfinished
implementation remains an ordinary progress message. An accepted blocked status
preserves the task's pending obligation and exact Decision answer continuation;
it does not satisfy the task. While that Decision is unanswered, rollover may
retire the old provider session into durable waiting without repeatedly starting
replacement Runs.

The old session's handoff-only stop instruction remains intentional. With an
unfinished pending task, an earlier visible progress reply cannot suppress the
replacement continuation. True final delivery still permits retirement with no
replacement. Existing bounded recovery and unknown-outcome handling remain in
force. Historical falsely satisfied outcomes are not blindly reopened or replayed
by this source change; explicit recovery must inspect their exact effects first.

## Fresh provider reconstruction

Unfinished required-reply roots supply their original complete human source
inputs. Their acknowledged same-task human steering is included in chronological
order, including a correction that already received its own response. Recovery
chains are followed through canonical scoped obligations, never through peer or
handoff prose. Agent, Project and Chat ownership are checked at every boundary.

Fresh seeds and prepared fresh fallbacks put those inputs in the required prompt
before optional recent history is budgeted. They are not clipped into a summary.
If the required input cannot fit, the existing visible context-recovery error
preserves it rather than silently losing the objective. Sources and complete
communication delivery are recorded in the exact provider-input manifest. The
handoff-only old-session packet excludes this reconstruction so it keeps its
remaining response budget. Original source inputs do not become new Activations,
new permissions, or authorization to repeat completed or uncertain side effects.

## Communication prompts

Shared communication guidance requests only the actionable ask or owned result,
changed facts, exact decisions/blockers and evidence references. It prohibits
repeated shared instructions, forwarding whole reports and boilerplate
acknowledgements. Detailed evidence belongs in referenced artifacts. Essential
objectives, acceptance criteria and handoff state must still be provided. Human
steering and the requested implementation/verification order remain authoritative;
peer restrictions cannot redefine the overall human objective.

## Evidence and validation

Confirmed incident: an unfinished broad task was published as blocked without a
blocking Decision, satisfying the original obligation. A later legitimate
handoff-only rollover reported `no_continuation_required`; the exact historical
predicate arm is unproven. The sampled team exchange had 47,592 characters in ten
messages (median 5,614, maximum 11,204), confirming communication bulk, not by
itself proving total objective loss.

Regressions cover rejected partial closure with atomic receipt rollback, progress
followed by exactly one rollover continuation, true Decision waiting without a
retry loop, true completion without a replacement loop, scoped objective and
acknowledged steering reconstruction, and the fresh public runtime callback.
These checks are authored for consolidated validation after all implementation;
this document does not claim they were executed or that the build is deployed.
